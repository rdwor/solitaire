A little patience.

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, building each suit from Ace to King.

Build the seven columns downward in alternating colors. You can move a card or a complete face-up sequence. Only Kings can fill an empty column.

The stock turns over three cards. Only the top waste card is playable. You may cycle through the stock as often as you like.

Click or tap a card, then a destination. You can also drag cards, or double-click a top card to move it to a foundation.

Use Space to draw, Ctrl / ⌘ + Z to undo, and Escape to clear your selection. Tab and Enter also work.

Deals are random and are not guaranteed winnable. Hints suggest legal moves, not a guaranteed solution.
